## What is proved

Everything below is a Lean 4 theorem (Lean v4.31.0, mathlib at the revision pinned in `lean/lake-manifest.json`), for dimension 3 and gauge group SU(2).

* The frozen statement `AdalyFormal.StatementLaneG.P428Target8` is proved by `AdalyFormal.M2RightInv.p428Target8` (module `RI6`). It formalises the lower bound on p. 428 of Balaban, "Propagators for lattice gauge theories in a background field", Commun. Math. Phys. 99 (1985) 389-434: a positive lower bound on the quadratic form independent of the renormalisation level and of the gauge field in the regularity class. Axioms: `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.
* The strongest form, `p428Target8Total` (module `TT_F`, lane `total_20261017`), fixes the constants before the cube factor and the thresholds, and also gives existence of every printed inverse. The earlier forms follow from it by four declared implications.
* Chain: FamilyTorus / Family  ->  Mixed  ->  Total  ->  P428Target8. The paper section on the chain and `CLAIMS.tsv` give the names.
* The encoded statement without the coverage hypothesis (every site and plaquette of the collar lies in a class cube) is false, and so is the version that grants existence of the inverses (for every cube factor c >= 10, every ell >= 2, every a > 0). These are theorems, with the disclosures listed in the paper.
* Positive and negative results on the p. 409 containment, the union-of-blocks class, the literal all-plaquette readings, and non-vacuity witnesses. See the paper and `CLAIMS.tsv`.

What the paper does not claim is listed in `paper/OPEN_ITEMS.md`, section D. Do not add claims from that list casually.

## The two gates

A claim is acceptable only when both gates pass.

1. Correctness gate: a clean Lean rebuild of the whole project (`REPRODUCE.sh`, which runs `lake build`) and the axiom check (`Check.lean` against `expected/axioms.expected.txt`). Every cited declaration must depend only on `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` (or on no axiom, for definitions). `sorryAx` anywhere is a failure. The scan for `sorry`, `admit`, `native_decide`, `axiom`, `unsafe`, `implemented_by` and `extern` in the project sources (comments and strings stripped) must find nothing. `tools/scan_forbidden.py` also runs a second pass over the raw text (nothing stripped) for `@[...]` and `attribute [...]` lists containing `implemented_by` or `extern`; any hit is printed as an `ATTR` line and fails the comparison with `expected/forbidden_tokens.expected.txt`. It also reads a char literal such as `'"'` correctly, so a quote character cannot start a phantom string that hides code. The allowed list is the five modules below and must not grow.
2. Statement audit: the Lean statement must say what the paper says it says. A proof gate cannot catch a wrong statement. Read the definitions, not the docstrings; check who chooses each parameter and what each quantifier ranges over; run non-vacuity witnesses. The paper's section on the statement audit lists the known deviations from the print.
